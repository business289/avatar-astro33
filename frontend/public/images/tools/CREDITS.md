under the [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/): free to use,
including on websites and commercially; modification is allowed; attribution is

Restrictions of that licence that apply to this site: do not sell the unaltered

them as part of a trademark, do not imply that the people shown endorse the
product, and do not show identifiable people in a bad light or an offensive way.

Each file was downloaded from Pexels, cropped and re-encoded as JPEG. The plain names are 960 x 492 card thumbnails; the `*-feature.jpg` files are 800 x 600 (4:3) crops of the same photographs for the homepage featured carousel.
No colour grading or other editing was
applied to the photographs; the dark navy tint is added in CSS in
`src/components/ToolCard.tsx`.
